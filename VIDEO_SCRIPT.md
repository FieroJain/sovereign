# SOVEREIGN — 2-Minute Demo Video Script
## lablab.ai "Transforming Enterprise Through AI" Hackathon 2026

---

**[0:00–0:08] — HOOK** *(voice only, no slides)*

> "Every AI agent inside your company right now is running without a security layer.
> Zero behavioral monitoring. Zero real-time protection.
> One malicious prompt away from disaster."

---

**[0:08–0:18] — THE PROBLEM** *(show a simple prompt injection text)*

> "This is a prompt injection attack.
> The hacker doesn't need your password.
> They need your AI agent to do their job for them.
> And right now — your agents will obey."

---

**[0:18–0:30] — INTRODUCE SOVEREIGN** *(show Tab 1, Live Monitor)*

> "This is SOVEREIGN.
> Three enterprise AI agents, running normally.
> Each one has a behavioral DNA —
> a learned fingerprint of exactly what it should do.
> EmailAgent: internal mail only.
> External POST? Never. Not once in 300 logged actions."

*[Gesture to the three green gauges, all showing low anomaly scores: 12, 9, 11]*

---

**[0:30–0:50] — THE ATTACK** *(click INJECT ATTACK button)*

> "I'm injecting the attack now.
> Watch the anomaly score."

*[Pause — gauges update, EmailAgent spikes]*

> "Eight... twelve... [attack hits] ...NINETY FOUR.
> SOVEREIGN's IsolationForest model detected a behavioral violation in 0.3 seconds.
> Not because of a keyword.
> Because of what the agent was *about* to do."

*[Show the red log entry, the pulsing red border, the explanation text]*

---

**[0:50–1:10] — THE RESPONSE** *(switch to Tab 2, click Generate Policy)*

> "Gemini AI generates a security policy.
> Real YAML. Real rules."

*[Show YAML appearing in the code block]*

> "Validated: normal traffic passes. Attack traffic: blocked.
> Deployed across 847 agents in 0.3 seconds."

*[Click Validate, show green checkmarks, click Deploy progress bar]*

> "Watch the replay — same attack.
> Blocked. Instantly. Permanently."

*[Show BEFORE/AFTER comparison boxes]*

---

**[1:10–1:25] — PHYSICAL ISOLATION** *(click PHYSICALLY ISOLATE NODE)*

> "For the most critical threats —
> SOVEREIGN sends a physical isolation command.
> The compromised node is severed from the network.
> At hardware level."

*[Show network graph, EmailAgent node goes grey, edges disappear]*

> "No hacker on earth can hack a disconnected cable."

---

**[1:25–1:40] — RESEARCH** *(switch to Tab 3)*

> "We tested 20 attack variants.
> Rule-based security: 40%.
> SOVEREIGN: 95%.
> 2.35 times better."

*[Point to the bar chart, then the amber-highlighted 'MISSED' rows in the table]*

> "Because we don't look for bad words.
> We look for bad *behavior*."

---

**[1:40–2:00] — CLOSE** *(return to main header)*

> "SOVEREIGN. The behavioral immune system for enterprise AI.
> Your agents work for you.
> We make sure they keep it that way."

*[Hold on the SOVEREIGN title with cyan glow and 'SECURE' status badge]*

---

## Recording Notes

- Record at 1920×1080, 60fps
- Use a browser zoom of 90% so all three agent columns are visible without scrolling
- Inject attack at exactly 0:30 for timing — practice the click
- Tab 2 steps are pre-loaded in order; have policy already generated in a second run to show the full flow quickly if time is tight
- The network graph animation is the most visually striking moment — hold on it for 3 seconds before moving on
- Background music: low-energy electronic/ambient, under 30% volume
